How to use Recipe Box

A walk-through of everything, tab by tab

Overview

Recipe Box is your own private recipe collection, with a few extras built in for actually cooking and sharing. You paste, link, or photograph a recipe and it gets turned into a tidy card — title, macros, ingredients, and steps. From there you can cook hands-free, build a grocery list, share recipes with family, and everything stays backed up and recoverable if you ever delete something by accident. Here's what each part of the app does.

🏠 Home

This is your recipe library. Every card shows the title, category tags, macros, and a heart to mark favorites. Tap a card to expand it and see the full ingredient list and instructions, along with buttons for Cook Mode, the grocery list, sharing, and more.

At the top, the filter pills let you narrow the view: All, Favorites, a category like Dinner or Dessert, and if your library has recipes from more than one person, you'll also see pills for Mine and each contributor's name.

➕ Add a recipe

Tap the orange plus button on the bottom bar. You can paste raw recipe text, paste a link to a recipe page, or upload a PDF — the app reads it and files it into the right categories automatically. You can also attach a photo, either by choosing one or dragging it in.

If a recipe doesn't list its macros, the app estimates them from the ingredients instead of leaving them blank — you'll see a small "estimated" note whenever that's happened.

📇 A recipe card, expanded

Tap any card to open it up. Along the bottom you'll find:

Cook Mode — a big-text, step-by-step view for actually cooking, with your phone screen staying awake.
Add to list — sends that recipe's ingredients straight to your grocery list.
Send to… — share this one recipe with someone in your contacts. It only joins their library once they accept it.
Add/Change photo — attach or replace the recipe's picture.
Share PDF — builds a proper one-page PDF and hands it to your phone's share sheet (Mail, Messages, AirDrop).
Print — opens your browser's normal print dialog.
Watch video / Add video link — if the recipe came from a video, this opens it. If it didn't, you can paste a link in yourself.
Remove recipe — moves it to Recently Deleted, not gone for good. You'll get an instant Undo too.

👨‍🍳 Cook Mode

One step at a time, in large text, so you're not squinting or scrolling with messy hands. Tap 🔊 Read aloud and it'll read each step out loud, then automatically keep reading as you move to the next one — tap it again anytime to turn it off. 🔁 Replay repeats the current step, and the speed buttons (0.75x–1.5x) slow it down or speed it up.

🛒 Grocery List

Found under Menu. Every ingredient you've sent here shows as a tappable item — tap to check it off as you shop. You can share the whole list as text, clear just the checked items, or clear everything.

👤 Profile

Your name and email, plus a stat summary of your library. Tap the pencil next to your name to fix a typo or change it anytime. Below that, the Full library / Start empty toggle sets the default for future invites — but you can always override it for a specific invite when you send one.

🎉 Invite

Enter someone's email, choose whether they get your full library or start empty, and it builds a personal invite link. Share it however you like. When they open it, they set their own name and password and get their own private library — connected to yours, but never mixing your recipes into theirs without your say-so.

📥 Shared with you

When someone sends you an individual recipe through "Send to…", it lands here first — not directly in your library. You choose Accept or Skip for each one.

👥 My Contacts

Everyone who's accepted an invite from you shows up here — this is who you can pick from when you tap "Send to…" on a recipe.

📲 Import recipes

For bringing in a batch of recipes from an export file (like from a video-to-recipe tool) — one recipe or a big batch at once. It automatically skips exact duplicates, and flags anything that looks similar-but-different so you can decide instead of it guessing. There's also a Find recipes to remove tool here if you ever need to undo a batch import, and a running history of every import you've done.

🗑️ Recently Deleted

Anything you remove — one recipe or everything at once — lands here first, not gone immediately. Each one shows exactly how many days are left (90 total) before it's permanently deleted. Tap Restore to bring it back, or Delete forever if you're sure.

📤💾 Backups

Two different exports, for two different jobs. Export all recipes (text) gives you a readable file you can look through or print. Download backup (restorable file) is the one that actually matters for safety — it saves a file you can keep anywhere (your computer, a cloud drive, wherever) and bring back in through Import recipes at any point in the future, completely independent of this account.
